Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

The short version
  • There's no account. We don't ask for your name, email or anything else about you.
  • When you ask for a thought, your photo is sent securely to write it, then it's forgotten. We don't store your photos or the thoughts.
  • No adverts, no analytics, no tracking.

Who we are

Furbrain is made by Tom Bettle, based in the United Kingdom. For anything about this policy or your data, email furbrain.help@icloud.com.

Photos you choose or take

When you take a photo or pick one from your library, the app shrinks it and sends it over an encrypted connection to our server so an AI model can write your pet's thought. Specifically:

  • Our server runs on Supabase. It passes the photo straight on and does not save it.
  • The thought is written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Anthropic receives the photo only to write the thought. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. It may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • The app only sends photos when you ask for a thought. It can't see the rest of your photo library: you choose one photo at a time.
  • Please avoid photos that show other people's faces or personal details. The app is for animals.

Camera

The app asks to use your camera so you can take a photo of your pet. Photos taken in the app are handled in the same way as above and aren't saved to your library unless you choose to save the card.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a count of how many thoughts you've had today, so it can apply the daily limit. This count stays on your phone and is removed if you delete the app.

Technical information

Like most online services, our hosting provider may briefly record technical details of each request, such as the time and IP address, to keep the service running and secure. We don't use this to identify you, and we don't combine it with anything else.

Where your data is processed

Supabase and Anthropic may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved for transfers of personal data.

Children

Furbrain is a light-hearted app suitable for everyone, but it isn't designed for children under 13 to use on their own. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we don't keep photos or accounts, we usually hold nothing that identifies you, but we're happy to help: email furbrain.help@icloud.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
