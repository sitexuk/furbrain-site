About

A small app with one job.

Every pet owner has looked at their dog sprawled across the sofa, or their cat glaring from the top of the fridge, and wondered what on earth is going through their head.

Furbrain answers that, one photo at a time. Take a picture, and the app writes a short, dry, first-person thought about exactly what it sees: the pose, the expression, the toy they definitely didn't ask for. It's meant to make you laugh and to be shared, nothing more.

How the thoughts are written

Thoughts are written by an AI model that looks at your photo. It's for fun, not a real reading of your pet's mind, though we suspect it's often right.
