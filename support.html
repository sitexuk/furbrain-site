Support

Something not right? Start here.

Contact us: furbrain.help@icloud.com. We usually reply within a couple of days.

Common questions

Why does it say "No pet spotted"?

The app couldn't see an animal in the photo. Try a photo where your pet is clearly visible and not too far away. Soft toys, drawings and people don't count, however cute.

Why does it say "That's all for today"?

You get 10 AI thoughts on your first day, then 3 a day for free. Furbrain Plus, a one-off purchase, gives you 30 a day. New thoughts are available each day. Ralph and Mr Pickles are always free.

Why does it say "Mind reading failed"?

The app couldn't reach our server, usually because of a weak internet connection. Check you're online and tap Try again. A failed attempt doesn't count towards your 10 a day.

The camera button doesn't work

You may have said no when the app asked to use the camera. To turn it back on, open the iPhone Settings app, scroll down to Furbrain and switch Camera on. You can still choose photos from your library without it.

How do I turn the morning thought on or off?

On the app's home screen, use the Morning thought switch. It sends one reminder at 9am. If the switch won't stay on, notifications are turned off for Furbrain: open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Furbrain and allow them.

I bought Furbrain Plus on another phone

Tap Furbrain Plus at the bottom of the home screen, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I share a thought?

Tap Share under the card. You can send it to Messages, WhatsApp or Instagram, or save it to Photos.

Is the thought really what my pet is thinking?

It's written by an AI for fun, so treat it as entertainment. That said, if the cap fits.

Do you keep my photos?

No. Your photo is sent securely to write the thought and isn't stored by us. The privacy policy has the details.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, email or password.
